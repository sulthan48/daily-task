About

Sushant Singh Rajput was born on 21 January 1986 in Patna, Bihar He was an Indian actor known for his talent, intelligence and love for science. He started his career in television and later becameone of Bollywood's most popular actors.

Education

He studied at St. Karen's High School in Patna and later at Kulachi Hansraj Model School in New Delhi.He secured All India Rank 7 in the Delhi College of Engineeringentrance examination and joined Mechanical Engineering.However, he left engineering to pursue acting.

Career

Sushant began his acting career in television with "Kis Desh Mein Hai Meraa Dil".His breakthrough came with "Pavitra Rishta".In 2013 he made his Bollywood debut with "Kai Po Che!".He later starred in several successful films such as PK, MS Dhoni: The Untold Story, Kedarnath,Chhichhore and Dil Bechara.

Failures & Challenges

Like many actors, Sushant faced failures in his career.Some films including Raabta and Drive did not perform well at the box office.Several movie projects were delayed or cancelled.Despite these setbacks, he continued improving himself and chose challenging roles.
