Introduction

Structural analysis is the process of studying how buildings, bridges and other structures behave under different loads.

Types of Structures

Beams

Carry loads horizontally and transfer them to supports.

Columns

Vertical members that transfer loads to foundations.

Trusses

Framework structures made with connected members.

Types of Loads

  • Dead Load
  • Live Load
  • Wind Load
  • Earthquake Load
